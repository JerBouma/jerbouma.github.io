Dependence ties the variables together: it measures how every variable's historical changes have moved alongside every other one's, shrinks those correlations toward zero where the shared history is short ([Schafer and Strimmer, 2005](https://doi.org/10.2202/1544-6115.1175){:target="_blank"}), and turns them into the one correlation matrix the simulation draws its linked shocks from. [Simulation Engine](/projects/financescenarios/docs/simulation-engine#how-the-correlations-are-estimated) explains the estimate in full.
