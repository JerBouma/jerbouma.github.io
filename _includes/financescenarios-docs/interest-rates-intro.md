Interest rates discount liabilities, drive bond returns and set the risk-free rate every equity earns on top of. In the default model each currency has two rates that move together the way central banks and bond markets make them move: a short rate that follows a monetary-policy rule on the simulated inflation and unemployment, starting from the path the bond market expects, and a 10-year rate built on that short rate as its expected path plus a term premium. Other models are options, from Hull-White and CIR to the joint models of rates and inflation: KNW, fitted on history, and DNB's CP2022, with the parameters DNB publishes every quarter.
